How to Generate Your Test Token

1
Sign into Account B

Log in to your secondary Raindrop.io account (e.g. in a private/incognito window or separate profile).

2
Open Integrations Settings

Visit Raindrop Settings > Integrations.

3
Create an App under "For Developers"

Scroll down to the For Developers section and click Create new app. Enter any name (e.g., Secondary Saver).

4
Generate Test Token

Click on your new app, click Create test token, copy the string, and paste it into the field above.
